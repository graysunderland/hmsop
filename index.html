The original CryptoPunks contract shipped with a bug.

June 2017. Larva Labs deployed the first CryptoPunks contract. A single line let buyers withdraw the funds from their own purchase — taking the punk and getting their ETH back. Sellers got nothing. The code couldn't be patched. Blockchain is forever. They deployed V2 two weeks later and airdropped fixed tokens to the original claimants. The V1 contract stayed broken. The V1 punks stayed permanent. 0x6Ba6f... still lives on Ethereum, unsealed, untouchable, the original sin and original truth of NFTs.

→ Our pixels work the same way. Once placed, a pixel cannot be un-placed — not by refund, not by transfer, not by failure. The art outlives the economics. The bug, ported forward as a feature.
FIND THE BUG · 1-IN-576 RARITY

Some pixels render as black squares — defects from the original contract.

Every mint has a 1-in-576 chance of placing a Bug pixel. The roll uses the same blockhash randomness that caused the original V1 exploit. Approximately 10 Bug pixels will exist at full mint. Bug pixels are permanent, named on-chain (isBug = true), and get a special tag in the patron ticker. They are the rarest, most desirable mints in the collection. You can't choose to mint a Bug. The bug chooses you.

01 — Mechanic
Every donation places one pixel, one layer deep.
A 24×24 canvas — same dimensions as a CryptoPunk. Each cell has 10 layers. Your blockhash decides the cell. First patron to land on a cell = palest shade. Tenth patron = full V1 lavender. 5,760 mints = exactly 10 layers per cell on average = a saturated portrait.
02 — Hold then sweep
Nothing is bought until 33 ETH is reached.
All donations are held by the smart contract — locked, untouchable, no human in the loop. When the pool reaches 33 ETH (current V2 floor), the contract begins sweeping V1 CryptoPunks from the floor, one by one, until the pool is spent. How many V1s we end up with depends on what the V1 market lets us steal.
03 — The Bug
pixels are rare. 1 in 576.
A homage to the V1 contract bug — a defect, immortalized in the art. Each mint has a 1-in-576 chance of getting a Bug pixel. ~10 expected at full mint. Bug pixels render as solid black squares. They are permanent on-chain rarities and tagged in the patron ticker as [BUG]. You can't mint one on purpose. The bug chooses you.
04 — Price
33 ETH ÷ 5,760 = 0.00573 ETH per mint.
Roughly $11 USD. Fixed at deploy — no bonding curve, no early-bird discount, no whales-only tier. Everyone pays the same. High enough that 5,760 patrons gets us to V2 floor money. Low enough for anyone with a wallet.
05 — Your NFT
Your token is the canvas at the block you minted.
A snapshot. With your pixel marked. Patron #1's piece shows a near-empty canvas. Patron #5,760's piece shows the finished portrait. 5,760 unique moments of the same artwork's becoming. All on-chain SVG — no IPFS, no external dependency. The token also stores your @handle and whether you got a Bug.
06 — Refunds
If we don't reach 33 ETH in 90 days, take your ETH back.
Refunds are trustless and permissionless. Call refund(). Your ETH returns. Your pixel stays on the canvas. Locked. Permanent. A record of the attempt. (See above — the bug we can't patch.)
