THE BUG · 1-IN-640 RARITY

The original CryptoPunks shipped with a bug. We honour it.

June 2017. A single line in Larva Labs' contract let buyers withdraw their own purchase funds — taking the punk and getting their ETH back. The code couldn't be patched. They deployed V2 two weeks later. 0x6Ba6f... still lives on Ethereum, unsealed and untouchable. The original sin and original truth of NFTs.

→ Every mint has a 1-in-640 chance of placing an Alien pixel — pale cyan, like the 9 Alien punks in the V1 collection. ~9 will exist at full mint. Visible only on Alien-holders' tokens. You can't choose to mint one. The bug chooses you.
01 — Mechanic
Every donation places one pixel, one layer deep.
A 24×24 canvas — same dimensions as a CryptoPunk. Each cell has 10 layers. Your blockhash decides the cell. First patron to land on a cell = palest shade. Tenth patron = full V1 lavender. 5,760 mints = exactly 10 layers per cell on average = a saturated portrait.
02 — Continuous sweep
The contract buys V1s as soon as it can afford them.
No big-bang event, no human deciding the moment. Every time the pool accumulates enough ETH for the current V1 floor (currently around 4 ETH), the contract automatically buys one V1 from the lowest listing. Then it keeps going. All the way up to 33 ETH total raised — the V2 floor. Each swept punk lands on the gallery above.
03 — Price
33 ETH ÷ 5,760 = 0.00573 ETH per mint.
Roughly $11 USD. Fixed at deploy — no bonding curve, no early-bird discount, no whales-only tier. Everyone pays the same. High enough that 5,760 patrons gets us to V2 floor money. Low enough for anyone with a wallet.
04 — The Aliens
pixels are rare. 9 in 5,760.
Each mint has a 1-in-640 chance of becoming an Alien — rendered in cyan, the same shade as the background of the 9 Alien CryptoPunks in the original collection. ~9 will exist at full mint. Aliens are tagged in the patron ticker as [ALIEN]. Only Alien-holders' tokens reveal Aliens. Every Alien minted before yours appears on your token in cyan. Non-Alien tokens show pure lavender. You can't choose to mint one. The bug chooses you.
05 — Your NFT
Your token is the canvas at the block you minted.
A snapshot. With your pixel marked. Patron #1's piece shows a near-empty canvas. Patron #5,760's piece shows the finished portrait. 5,760 unique moments of the same artwork's becoming. All on-chain SVG — no IPFS, no external dependency. The token stores your @handle and whether you got an Alien. If you did, your token shows every Alien minted before yours in cyan. Other tokens show pure lavender.
06 — Refunds
If we don't reach 33 ETH in 90 days, take your ETH back.
Refunds are trustless and permissionless. Call refund(). Your ETH returns. Your pixel stays on the canvas. Locked. Permanent. A record of the attempt. (Note: only the un-spent portion of the pool is refundable. ETH already used to sweep V1s belongs to the collective treasury.)
